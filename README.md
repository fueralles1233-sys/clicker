# 🕹️ Stardance Clicker - Ultimate Edition

Welcome to **Stardance Clicker**, a feature-rich, highly addictive incremental idle game built entirely with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript!

## 🚀 Amazing Features
*   **Deep Upgrade System:** Buy Dance Offices and unlock powerful upgrades to boost your passive income.
*   **Career Rebirth Multiplier:** Reset your stats for a permanent **+25% multiplier** on all future clicks to supercharge your career!
*   **Achievement Tracker:** Unlock special milestones as you play and track your progress live.
*   **Custom Button Skins:** Personalize your gameplay with **10 unlockable visual skins** for your main clicking button.
*   **Pro Keyboard Shortcuts:** Play faster using optimized hotkeys (**S** for Statistics, **P** for Shop, and **Spacebar** to click!).
